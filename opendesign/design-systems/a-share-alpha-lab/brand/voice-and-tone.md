# Voice And Tone

## Voice

Precise, numerate, and candid. The product speaks like a senior quant researcher sitting next to the investor: direct conclusions first, then assumptions and evidence.

## Copy Rules

- Use verbs such as `rank`, `confirm`, `block`, `rebalance`, `explain`, `stress`.
- Use numeric confidence when a signal appears.
- Name uncertainty explicitly: data lag, low liquidity, crowding, high event risk.
- Avoid promotional language, guarantees, and vague labels like "smart recommendation."
- Prefer "model signal" or "research candidate" over "buy recommendation."

## Formatting

- Tickers: `300750.SZ`, `688111.SH`.
- Horizons: `2-8W`, `20D`, `60D`.
- Scores: one decimal place, e.g. `82.4`.
- Percentages: signed where relevant, e.g. `+7.8%`.
- Risk labels: `OK`, `Watch`, `Blocked`, `Reduce`.
